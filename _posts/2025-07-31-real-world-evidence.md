# Summary

RWE was believed to advance RWD and its benefits. In reality, there are multiple barriers. Policies were established to overcome these barriers. The following table summarizes them:

| Barrier                                                            | Approach                                        | Policies                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :----------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Data unrelated to compensation or reimbursement is poorly recorded | Improve capture at data source                  | {::nomarkdown}<ul><li>Provide compensation for physicians and health care organizations, such as providing CMS</li><li>Invest in AI that helps data capture</li></ul>{:/}                   |
| EHR does not provide complete, longitudinal data for individuals   | Enable access to longitudinal whole-person data | {::nomarkdown}<ul><li>Allow health data exchange by modifying TEFCA</li><li>Implement a national all-payer claims database</li><li>Invest in organizations that can link data</li></ul>{:/} |
| Regulations                                                        | Improve regulatory practices                    | {::nomarkdown}<ul><li>Train FDA reviewers and inspectors</li><li>Fund regulatory research such as application of AI in regulation</li></ul>{:/}                                             |
